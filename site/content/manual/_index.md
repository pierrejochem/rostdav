This manual is for the people who use a Rostdav server. If you run the server yourself, read the [administrator guide](@/admin/_index.md) as well.

Rostdav stores your files on a server that belongs to you or your organisation. You reach them in two ways: with the **web interface** in your browser, and with the **Nextcloud apps** (Android, iOS, desktop) that sync and open files from your phone or computer. Both show the same files.
